Rat audio assets

Squeak.wav, Scurry.wav, Release.wav: original synthesized game sound effects
created for this project by RatSetup.MakeSound. No third-party recordings used.
Landing: existing project Audio/Materials/PlasticTap.wav.

Mono 22050 Hz PCM, preloaded; two shared spatial ambient sources plus one
interaction source. Linear rolloff: 3m minimum, 8m maximum. Idle squeaks are
randomized every 25-40 seconds per rat, at most one every 15 seconds across the
colony, and restricted to the two nearest loose rats. Scurry patter repeats
every 0.8-1.3 seconds while a nearby rat runs. RatColony.volume (0.65) scales
every rat sound. Rats placed or tossed into the glass container are silent.
Global sound settings, pause, loss of focus and blocked gameplay silence voices.
